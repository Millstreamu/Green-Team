What to draw

One 128 × 96 px transparent PNG: four columns, three rows of 32px cells. Nine pieces, three spare cells.

Tile pieces & compatibility
  1. 00 Floor: opaque, repeating 32 × 32 tile.
  2. 01 Back wall: full 32px tile including its stone top and face.
  3. 02–03 Sides: narrow left/right walls. Follow the example's 6px strip; keep the rest transparent.
  4. 04 Front rim: a 6px strip along the top. No tall foreground slab.
  5. 05–08 Corners: outer NW, NE, SW and SE. Follow their alignment; leave 09–11 empty.

Fixed wall shapes keep all passage floors clear. Inside joins and ends combine these pieces. Earlier 128 × 64 and 128 × 128 sheets still import with their original rendering.

## Review outcome

Keep the current 25/50/75 XP ladder for this test. Bell is the cheapest new recipe and needs two material units; smoke/disguise offer different five/ten-second windows for the same three-unit craft; dart/orb sit at the higher recipe tier with distinct control/information roles and opposite scrap/cloth mixes. This is a consistency review, not proof of equal power. Existing reports confirm usability but do not establish acquisition/use/extraction rates. Repricing without those observations would be a guess.

Sneaking requires200XP if learning every recipe from a fresh profile, versus75XP for Distraction. Present it as a suggested later kit, not a required starter path. Existing gear can be packed without owning its recipe. Players should not need all three kits unlocked to progress.

Packed Lunch is the concrete gap: team rescue can use stocked/test lunches but has no normal crafting recipe yet. The guide explicitly names that limitation rather than pricing an unavailable recipe. Defining its normal source/cost is the recommended next bounded balance decision; no new lunch recipe is silently added here.

Try each kit over a few runs. Note start/end material stock, which items are actually spent, whether they create an escape/loot opportunity, valuables recovered, and whether the three-square commitment leaves enough haul space. Include failed runs; compare solo with team play where applicable. Review recipe progression separately from repeat-use material cost. Keep timers/prices as trial values until this evidence supports a focused change.

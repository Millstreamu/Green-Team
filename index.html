Controls & tips

Phone sideways: touch and drag anywhere on the left to move or aim a trap. E interacts (hold to search/hide/place). Use activates equipped gear. Select a sleep dart or Clang bell and E to equip; move freely, then Use enters aim and a second Use fires or throws; Cancel returns to equipped. Equip Lucky and Use to drink: +5 percentage points gold-coin finding chance in your completed searches for this run; doses do not stack. Equip Vanish and Use to drink: eight seconds of invisibility, ending on a hit, searching or gear use. Equip a guard disguise potion, then Use to drink: ten seconds, guards within one tile recognise you; searching or using gear breaks it. Equip a scrying orb and Use to mark the three closest guards on everyone’s map for five seconds. Purple diamonds show where they were when pinged. Find meat and berries in cupboards/crates; bring them home and craft Packed Lunch at the workbench. Select berries and E equips; Use aims, then Use throws. A hit blinds a guard for one second and breaks chase. Throwing spends one portion. Sack opens inventory. Drag sack items to move; tap to highlight, then Rotate to turn them. E equips selected gear. Move explicitly lifts an item for dropping or sharing; Cancel stops trap placement.
